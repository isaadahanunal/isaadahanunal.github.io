Game Roadster — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Game Roadster ("the app") is developed by İsa Adahan Ünal. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app.

Information the app stores

Your game progress (scores, stars, credits, unlocked cars, upgrades and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run servers, do not require an account, and do not collect, sell or share this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, diagnostics, and interactions with ads.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission to track via Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: they are only shown when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
